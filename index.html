How We Work

A clear system that protects children from blame and keeps parents informed.

Many children hesitate to ask doubts in school or large coaching classes. When marks drop, parents naturally worry and scold the child. We replace guesswork with three simple rules.

STEP 01 · BEFORE ADMISSION

Baseline Level Check

Before starting tuition, we conduct a calm, one-on-one diagnostic check in reading, handwriting, and basic maths. This shows us—and you—the child's exact current level so they are never compared unfairly or scolded for older gaps.

DURATION: 20 MINS NO FEE
STEP 02 · FIRST TWO DAYS

2-Day Free Demo Classes

Your child attends two full 1.5-hour sessions with their batch at zero cost. Observe how comfortably they ask questions, complete their school diary homework, and settle into the environment before you decide on admission.

2 × 1.5 HR SESSIONS ZERO OBLIGATION
STEP 03 · EVERY SATURDAY

Weekly Test on Parent's Phone

Every Saturday is test day on everything taught Monday through Friday. We check the paper and send a clear photo of the answer sheet, marks, and specific remarks straight to your WhatsApp number.

FREQUENCY: WEEKLY DIRECT WHATSAPP
Daily Structure

How every 90-minute (1.5 hour) batch is used.

Unstructured tuition wastes time copying pages without understanding. We divide every 90-minute session into three timed blocks so school homework and core learning both finish on time.
MINUTES 00 – 40 40 Mins

School Diary & Daily Homework

We check the school almanac first. All daily school homework, workbook exercises, and classwork gaps are completed neatly so parents don't have to sit with notebooks late at night.

MINUTES 40 – 70 30 Mins

Concept Teaching & Written Practice

Focused chapter explanation in Maths, Science/EVS, or English Grammar. Children solve practice sums and write answers independently while the tutor checks step-by-step logic.

MINUTES 70 – 90 20 Mins

Oral Tables, Reading & Dictation

The final 20 minutes are dedicated to loud reading (English/Hindi), spelling dictation, and multiplication tables so retention stays strong for Saturday's written test.
